# ADHD-EEG Research

This repository contains the complete research journey of our ADHD-EEG project, from the initial understanding of the research paper to experiments, findings, and the final research paper.

## Objective

To study ADHD using EEG data, reproduce the selected research methodology, investigate possible improvements, and develop a research contribution suitable for publication.

## Research Record

This repository records:

- Initial domain and paper understanding
- Dataset and preprocessing
- Methodology and implementation
- Experiments and results
- Research decisions and findings
- Failed or abandoned approaches
- Final research work

## Team Contributions

The repository also preserves each team member's actual contributions through code, research, documentation, experiments, analysis, and Git history.

This allows every member to demonstrate their individual work and contribution in resumes and interviews.

## Repository Structure

- `papers/` — Research papers
- `docs/` — Research documentation
- `data/` — Dataset-related files and information
- `notebooks/` — Exploration and analysis
- `src/` — Source code
- `experiments/` — Experiments and results
- `tests/` — Tests
- `figures/` — Research figures
- `archive/` — Deprecated or abandoned work

> **The goal is to record the evolution of the research, not just the final result.**
